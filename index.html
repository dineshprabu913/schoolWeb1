Our approach

We teach the kid in front of us, not the average of the class.

Northwood has been a neighbourhood school since 1962 — small enough that teachers eat lunch with families they've known for a decade, and ambitious enough that our grade 8s are building robots and arguing about ethics in the same semester.

A

Ask before you tell

Lessons open with a question, not a definition. Kids arrive at the vocabulary themselves.

B

Small enough to notice

Class sizes stay under 22 so no one finishes a year unseen.

C

Hands stay busy

Every grade has a standing shop, garden, or studio block — not just an assembly once a term.

Campus life

Outside the timetable

Sport

Cross-country, basketball, and a badminton club that somehow outdraws the football team.

Arts

A student-run gallery in the front hallway, repainted every term by the grade currently showing.

Making

Wood shop, sewing room, and a robotics team that ships an entry to regionals every March.

Outdoors

A working garden plot per homeroom, and an annual three-day canoe trip for grades 7–8.
